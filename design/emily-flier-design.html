"Emily" — Daily Station Flier

Design for review · nothing built yet · our take on draftdsp.com/fliers/carl, named Emily
Assumption to confirm: I couldn't open the Carl page (their site blocks robots), so this design reads it as: a one-click daily flier a dispatcher prints / posts / drops in the group chat before waves — a friendly "morning brief" persona with today's key info, recognition, and a safety focus. If Carl does something different (e.g. recruiting fliers or incident posters), tell me and I'll re-cut this.

"Copy as image" renders the flier to a PNG in the browser — paste straight into the DA group chat. Print gives a clean A4. A /emily/today URL shows the latest flier full-screen for a wall TV or iPad by the door.

4 · Storage

One small table station_flier (FLIER_DATE, SAFETY_FOCUS, ANNOUNCEMENT, WEATHER_LINE, SHOUTOUTS_JSON, audit) — one row per day, so yesterday's flier is one click to clone, and the flier history doubles as a stand-up log.
