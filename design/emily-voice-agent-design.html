Finding 2 — two things live outside MVPx, and one of them needs your action

The hard constraint: the voice layer (telephony + speech + turn-taking) cannot run inside Tomcat. It's a cloud service (Vapi, as your spec suggests) that must reach MVPx over the public internet via webhooks. Your Tomcat is on a local Windows box — so we need a secure tunnel (Cloudflare Tunnel, free, stable, no port-forwarding) exposing ONLY /MVPx/api/emily/* with a shared-secret header. This is standard, but it's the one piece of new infrastructure, and the Vapi account + phone number are yours to create (I can't sign up for services on your behalf — setup is ~30 minutes with my step-by-step).
Rough running cost to sanity-check the business case: Vapi-style stacks land around $0.10–$0.20 per call-minute all-in (telephony + speech + LLM). A station doing 80 calls/day at ~2 min avg ≈ $16–$32/day. Cheap next to dispatcher hours, but real money — worth confirming before Phase 3+.

Finding 3 — trims I recommend to your spec for the MVP

  • 12 intents → 8 at launch. Merge PACKAGE_DAMAGE / MISSORT / RETURN_OTHER into one PACKAGE_EXCEPTION intent with a category slot (same logging path); merge PHONE/CABLE/FLEX into DEVICE_ISSUE with a type slot. Fewer intents = higher first-utterance accuracy on noisy van audio.
  • WhatsApp out, SMS in. WhatsApp Business API approval is weeks of friction; Twilio SMS is a day. Rescue details go by SMS in the MVP.
  • QA call replay ships free — Vapi stores recordings + transcripts; the console just links them. Don't build audio storage.
  • Warm transfer needs a target: "under 15 seconds" requires a dispatcher line that answers. Decide: dispatch desk number, on-call cell rotation, or simultaneous-ring group. This is an ops decision, not code.
  • Recording consent: the greeting should include "this call may be recorded." NJ is one-party consent, but say it anyway — drivers may be from anywhere. (Confirm with counsel; not legal advice.)

How the pieces talk (one call):
1Driver dials the dispatch number → Vapi answers instantly with the greeting (supported topics + "say emergency now").
2Vapi calls our webhook POST /api/emily/context with the caller number → MVPx matches contact.MOBILE → returns driver, today's route/staging, pace snapshot, checkin state. Emily now greets by name and expects the right route number.
3Conversation runs. When Emily wants to DO anything she calls a tool → our endpoint: /api/emily/rts-check, /log-exception, /rescue-candidates, /escalate. Each returns an explicit allow/deny/result object; Emily just voices it.
4Emergency word at ANY point → Vapi warm-transfers to the dispatch line, passing a spoken + SMS summary; MVPx logs a P1 escalation that flashes in the Emily Console.
5Call ends → Vapi end-of-call webhook → MVPx stores transcript, intent, outcome, confidence, duration in emily_calls. Everything Emily wrote is tagged SOURCE='EMILY'.
